# CLAUDE.md

- Do not propose watching or monitoring CI, a pull request or its checks, and do not offer to subscribe to their activity.
- Do not put a prefix such as `feat:` or `fix:` in the title of a pull request.
- Do not use wording such as "real device" or "physical device" in replies, code, comments, documents, commit messages or pull request descriptions; say what was actually done instead, for example that the tests ran or that the page was opened in the editor.
